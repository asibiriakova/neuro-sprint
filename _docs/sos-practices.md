## Distress zone (`panic_overarousal`) — Overarousal, Panic

Goal: rapid downregulation of the sympathetic nervous system, calming the amygdala, and returning control to the prefrontal cortex.

### 1. Physiological Sigh
- **Duration:** 2 minutes (5–7 cycles)
- **Protocol:**
  1. Inhale deeply through the nose.
  2. Take a short second inhale through the nose to fill the lungs fully.
  3. Exhale long and slow through the mouth.
  4. Repeat for 5–7 cycles.
- **Effect:** Immediate drop in heart rate via vagus nerve stimulation.

### 2. Sensory Grounding 5-4-3-2-1
- **Duration:** 3 minutes
- **Protocol:**
  1. Find 5 blue or square objects in your surroundings.
  2. Touch 4 different textures.
  3. Notice 3 background sounds.
  4. Notice 2 smells.
  5. Notice 1 physical taste.
- **Effect:** Shifts focus from mental rumination to the primary sensory cortex.

### 3. Cold Vagal Stimulation
- **Duration:** 1–2 minutes
- **Protocol:**
  1. Submerge your face in cool water, or press an ice cube / cold damp towel against your cheekbones and neck.
  2. Hold your breath for 10–15 seconds while doing so.
- **Effect:** Activates the "dive reflex," sharply reducing cortisol and arousal.

## Burnout zone (`apathy_freeze`) — Apathy, Passivity

Goal: gentle somatic activation without depleting pressure on the dopamine system.

### 4. Micro Neuro-Charge (Shaking)
- **Duration:** 3 minutes
- **Protocol:**
  1. Stand stably.
  2. Gently shake out your hands, shoulders, and legs.
  3. Do 3–4 gentle heel-to-toe rocks, emphasizing spinal lengthening.
- **Effect:** Restores proprioception, releases muscular tension, gently increases blood flow.

### 5. Sensory Contact ("Micro-Joy")
- **Duration:** 5 minutes
- **Protocol:**
  1. Prepare a glass of water or a hot tea.
  2. Drink it with full sensory presence — the mug's temperature, its aroma, the tactile sensation.
  3. No screens, no background noise during this time.
- **Effect:** Activates the insular cortex (interoception), returning awareness to the body.

### 6. Brain Dump
- **Duration:** 4 minutes
- **Protocol:**
  1. Write out the full stream of chaotic thoughts and worries — on paper or in a text field — without editing.
  2. When done, close or put away the page.
- **Effect:** Releases the Zeigarnik effect, freeing prefrontal working memory.
